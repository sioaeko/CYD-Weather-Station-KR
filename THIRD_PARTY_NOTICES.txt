This firmware adapts the weather-station page structure and touch interaction
from ThingPulse/esp8266-weather-station-color:
https://github.com/ThingPulse/esp8266-weather-station-color

The original ESP32/LittleFS adaptation is by Michael Fehr, with graphics
helpers and astronomy routines adapted by Bodmer from Daniel Eichhorn's work.
Their existing source notices and the repository LICENSE are retained.
The new landscape geometry follows the user-provided HTML reference.

Korean edition (CYD 날씨 스테이션 – 한국판)

Based on Rau7han/ESP32-CYD-Weather-Station v3.1
https://github.com/Rau7han/ESP32-CYD-Weather-Station
The Korean edition adds a portrait Korean UI, Korean location presets, KST/NTP
settings, Open-Meteo particulate matter data, Korean grade tables, daily advice,
a screen on/off schedule, a Korean font generator and a PC preview harness.

CYD_Weather_KR/KoreanFonts.h contains bitmap glyphs rendered from
NanumGothic Bold, Copyright (c) 2010 NAVER Corporation,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
https://openfontlicense.org  /  https://hangeul.naver.com/font
The font may be used, embedded and redistributed with software; the font
software itself may not be sold by itself.

Weather data: OpenWeather (https://openweathermap.org), CC BY-SA 4.0 data terms.
Air quality / UV data: Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com), CC BY 4.0,
based on Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) information.
